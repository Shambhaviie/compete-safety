### Understanding Credit Card Theft

**How it Works (Defensive Analysis)**
Cybercriminals employ various methods to steal credit card information, often referred to as "carding." These methods generally fall into three categories:

1.  **Phishing and Social Engineering**: Attackers create deceptive emails or websites that mimic legitimate financial institutions. When victims enter their card details on these fake sites, the information is captured and sold on dark web marketplaces.
